Our Story

Building South Africa's Future

Mcvigar Sand Mining (Pty) Ltd regards its responsibility to help bring about the empowerment of previously disadvantaged South Africans as integral to being a model corporate citizen and a truly South African company.

We endorse government led initiatives in this arena and wholeheartedly support the development of the mining, construction, manufacturing and farming sectors across our beautiful nation.

Our ongoing involvement in building iconic structures, farms, bridges, dams, airports and roads forms the backbone of many economies in developing countries, creating lasting impact for generations to come.

Vision

Top-Quartile Performance

Mcvigar Sand Mining (Pty) Ltd aims to own and operate a portfolio of infrastructure, mining, farming and manufacturing-related businesses, each of which will achieve top-quartile performance compared to its peers when measured against return on invested capital, earnings growth, and positive cash flow generation through the business cycle.

Our Commitment

Transformation Objectives

We're committed to driving meaningful transformation across all sectors we operate in.

Transform and grow sectors
To achieve a substantial change in the racial and gender composition of the ownership, control and management within the sector
To address the critical skills shortage and skills development with a specific focus on women
To enhance entrepreneurial development and sustainable development of Black Economic Empowered (BEE) Small, Medium and Micro Enterprise (SMME) construction, farming, mining and manufacturing companies through strategic partnerships
To bring an end to the malpractice of fronting in the industry
